# Code of conduct

This policy covers repository issues, pull requests, reviews and other
project spaces. Himanshu Kumar (@himanshu748) and Priyanshu Jha (@CodewithJha)
maintain it together.

Be considerate and specific. Welcome questions and contributions regardless
of background, identity, experience or ability. Critique the code and evidence;
explain disagreements and accept corrections. Respect consent and privacy.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexual attention, impersonation,
doxxing and deliberate disclosure of secrets or private recordings are not
acceptable. Do not retaliate against someone for raising a concern.

For a non-sensitive concern, open a GitHub issue in
[the repository](https://github.com/CodewithJha/rasta-voice/issues) and identify
the relevant public discussion. Public issues are visible to everyone: never
include private identities, contact details, recordings, credentials or other
sensitive evidence. Ask a maintainer there to arrange a private contact path
before sharing such evidence. No private reporting inbox is currently listed.
If the concern involves a maintainer, ask the other maintainer to handle it.

Maintainers should acknowledge reports, hear the relevant accounts and explain
the outcome to those involved without exposing private information. Depending
on severity and repetition, they may request an edit, remove harmful content,
warn the contributor, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation.
Anyone affected may ask the other maintainer to reconsider. Safety and privacy
take precedence over continuing an argument in public.
