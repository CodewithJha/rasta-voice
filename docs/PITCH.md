# Rasta Voice

**Speak the delivery update. Hear the next step.**

## The problem

A delivery rider reaches a closed gate. The customer does not answer. The rider needs help, while dispatch needs the order number, the delay and a clear next action.

## The product

Rasta Voice turns a short Hinglish update into delivery information and a spoken response. A rider sends a recording, upload or illustrative sample. Dispatch gets the issue, order identifier, ETA, urgency and suggested next step. The original transcript stays visible. The separate dispatcher workspace is passcode protected.

## The experience

In the recorded example, the rider reports a closed gate and a ten-minute wait for order eighteen. Rasta Voice identifies customer unreachable and suggests asking dispatch to call once and log the attempt. The rider taps Hear dispatch to hear the next step. Unclear order numbers prompt confirmation.

## Built with Gnani

Prisma v3.0 powers transcription through REST and streaming. Timbre v3.0 generates the rider example, spoken responses and demo narration. The 2:02 video shows the current deployed interface. Its rider message is synthetic, with real Gnani responses; it makes no field-accuracy claim.
